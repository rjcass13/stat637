Even though I currently have a job, I actually found the workshop really handy. I've gone to career fairs in the past and never really knew what to do or how to talk to the recruiters. However, during this workshop, I especially practiced my elevator pitch. It was helpful to see the general structure, as I think in the past when I've thought about elevator pitches I just thought of it as a quick version of my resume. However, we were taught that it should include more about where we're going/what we're hoping to get out of the conversation. Due to this, I refined my pitch to be a bit more brief about my experience, and include more about what I hope to accomplish in the future.
